Our Broth

The long way round, on purpose.

Bones, water, time. We simmer for fifteen hours with carrots, celery, onion and a splash of apple cider vinegar to coax the collagen out of the bone — then bay, rosemary, cardamom, turmeric and cinnamon go in for warmth.

What comes out is thick enough to set when it cools. That wobble is the collagen, and it is the whole point. Drink it warm on its own, or build a soup, a rice, a freekeh or a stew on top of it.

Family nourishment, everyday wellness — for skin and joints, muscle, and a gut that behaves.

Questions

Good to know

Does it need refrigerating?

Not while it is sealed. The jar is shelf stable and lives happily in a cupboard for up to 12 months from the production date printed on the label. Once you open it, keep it in the fridge and finish it within 5 days.

Are there really no preservatives?

None. The jar is filled hot and vacuum sealed, which is what keeps it safe — not chemicals. That is also why the lid button matters: it is the seal doing the work.

How do I serve it?

Warm it gently in a pan or the microwave and drink it as it is — that is how most people take it. It also makes a serious base for soup, rice, freekeh, stews and sauces. Do not boil it hard; a gentle heat keeps the texture.

Why does it set like jelly in the jar?

That is the collagen, and it is the sign of a proper long simmer. It melts back to liquid the moment it warms.

Is it halal?

Yes — both the beef and the chicken are halal certified, and the product is non-GMO.
